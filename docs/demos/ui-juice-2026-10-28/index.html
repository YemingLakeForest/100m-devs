SOUND LOG

1THREE MATERIALS, NOT ONE OUTLINE

Plate (filled, slab-shadowed) holds things, inset (darker, flush) shows readings, lit (solid phosphor) is the one thing that matters now. Outline stops carrying meaning, so the eye can rank the screen.

2KEYCAPS, ROLE-COLOURED

Every verb is a physical key on a hard slab: it drops 4px on press down (F2), snaps back with one frame of overshoot. Cyan = work, amber = money, red = danger, grey = neutral. Brackets retire.

3ONE SOUND PER VISIBLE STEP

The window unrolls in four heights, so it makes four clacks, falling in pitch; it closes in two, rising. Sound and picture share one clock, so they read as one object instead of an effect plus a noise.

4THE PENTATONIC LADDER

Mashing CODE climbs C D E G A and up an octave, then resets after 700 ms of rest. A combo you can hear, in the key of the existing blip family, with no wrong notes.

5MONEY TRAVELS

SHIP sends one aggregated chip from the key to the CASH cell in four hard hops. The cell does not change until it lands; it then counts up in steps (a tick per step, rising) and flashes white, amber, rest.

6DENIAL IS A SOUND AND A SHAKE

Press HIRE with no money: the key jolts, the COST plate flashes red, a two-note low buzz. Unlock is the opposite: a rising chord, the key beckons in two hard states. Nothing is ever silently disabled.

7HOLD, DON'T CONFIRM

Dangerous actions fill under your finger in stepped blocks with a climbing tone and drain with a falling one. It replaces a confirm dialog with a gesture, and the audio is the confirmation.

8VOICE BUDGET

Your press is always full volume; automation is quieter by 1/√devs and never more than one voice per 80 ms. Hover ticks are throttled and sit an octave above the clack. It scales to 1,000,000 like the throughput bed does.

Held back on purpose: no glow, bloom, blur, gradient light, eased fade or rounded corner (ART_DIRECTION §3, and your note on gradient light boxes). Every animation here is steps() between palette colours, JUICE OFF removes all of it but not the sound, and MOTION → REDUCED in the menu does the same thing in the real game.
