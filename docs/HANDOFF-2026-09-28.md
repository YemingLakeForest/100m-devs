## 2 · The direction: a SimCity 4 grid, and houses fall on thrusters

The canon is GDD §7.7.2's amendment. In short: past the garage, the world is square blocks between streets, seen from
the fixed camera; the garage is the first block; **one building to a block**; the studio grows by the next empty
block taking a building; **houses fall first**, landing the way a StarCraft Terran building does; the deadpan
register is unchanged.

**The landing, proposed (about 1.5 s a building):**

1. **Marker.** When a building is on its way, its block's outline glows, like StarCraft's placement grid, and
   tightens as it comes down.
2. **Fall, 0–0.4 s.** Fast, from well above the frame, trailing a little smoke.
3. **Ignition, 0.4 s.** The thrusters under it light at once, with a flash for the bloom; it squashes as the thrust
   catches it.
4. **Braking, 0.4–1.2 s.** Eased hard. Within about 4 m of the ground the exhaust blows dust sideways off the block.
5. **Hover, 1.2–1.5 s.** Flickering jets, a small drift to line up, landing legs out of the corners.
6. **Touchdown, 1.5 s.** Jets cut with a sputter, steam from the legs, the existing dust ring, a soft squash about a
   third of today's, a clunk rather than a whump.
7. **Mass hire.** Houses wait over their blocks on their jets and come down in turn.
8. **No clipping.** A flame is never longer than the gap under it; nozzles and legs stay inside the building until
   the last moment.

**Build order, proposed:**

1. `three/render/cityGrid.ts`: the layout, pure. Block size, street width, the order blocks fill (nearest the garage
   first), block to world position. Tests pin the claims: no two blocks overlap, fill order is nearest-first, and the
   garage's block is the origin.
2. `three/render/thrusters.ts`: one shared rig. Nozzles plus two additive cones each (an orange outer flame and a
   white-hot core, palette colours), flicker, flame length clamped to the gap below. The prestige lift-off and James's
   pond rocket reuse it.
3. The first falling house: the houses already standing around the garage (the ones `clearLot` hides) are
   three.js models and can be it.
4. `three/render/garageView.ts`: replace the lane with the grid. Headcount to buildings to blocks; frame the occupied
   blocks with `fitTo`, as `frameStudio` does now; exhaust dust through the existing puffs; an `ignitions` counter
   beside `landings` for the thruster sound.
5. Delete `three/render/laneStoreys.ts` once the grid stands, or keep its storey as the inside of a later, denser
   building. Decide then.
6. Sound: a thruster roar and a clunk are new SFX, and ElevenLabs is used only with the user's go. Reuse existing ones
   until then.
7. Amend GDD §7.7.2 again for whatever the open questions settle, and check it by looking: shots at each beat of the
   landing, and a close-up of a block's neighbours during a mass hire.

## 5 · Traps found today

- **The auto-mode classifier refuses edits to the gate and sweeping deletions** ("CI Bypass", "Irreversible Local
  Destruction"). Do not work around it: say what was refused, and the user can allow it through `/permissions`.
- **`npx tsc -b` runs from the repo root.** From `src/` there is no tsconfig, and it fails as if the code were wrong.
- **`batchArt` (`three/render/worldArt.ts`) leaves a box unbatched when its colour is nobody else's**, in the person's
  own group. Anything that hides or moves a seat's instances has to hide or move that group too, or a lone haircut
  floats over an empty desk (`laneStoreys.ts`, `show` and `move`).
- **`garageView.ts`'s seat sweep clears the planters on every call.** People leaving one at a time need the per-seat
  `vacate(seat)`, with the planters swept once the last one has gone.

## 6 · How to test hiring

`npm run dev`, then `http://127.0.0.1:5173/?notitle&scenarios`: type 20 in the headcount box and press Enter, set
BATCH to +1 and press add (the move), then +100 and +1K. The add button hires through the real arrival path, so it
animates. `?notitle&devs=500` or `?notitle&scenario=floor` jump straight to a headcount, already built. Add `&nopost`
to turn the CRT off. Loopback only.
